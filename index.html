Questions neighbors ask first

Is Local Mover Denver a moving company?

No. It is a free referral service run by Eye To Ad Media in Denver. We pass your request to up to three local movers we checked ourselves. The mover you pick does the work and writes the contract.

How do I check that a Denver mover is permitted?

Local moves that start and end in Colorado are regulated by the Colorado Public Utilities Commission. Ask for the mover's PUC household goods permit number, which should appear in its ads as CO PUC Mover Permit No. HHG-plus-digits, and confirm it with the PUC.

What does a mover's Colorado contract have to include?

Under C.R.S. 40-10.1-505 it must be written, signed and dated by both sides, and show the mover's name, address and phone, the move date, your addresses, itemized rates and services, at least two accepted forms of payment, and the PUC permit statement and number.

How do local movers usually charge?

Mostly by the hour for a crew and truck, often with a minimum number of hours or a starting fee. Published guides put typical Denver local rates around 115 dollars an hour for a crew and truck, but prices vary by company, season and building access.
