How this works, with no fine print hidden

Local Mover Denver is a free matching service. Here is the whole path from your form to a phone call.

  1. You send the form

    Name, phone and the basics of your move. Email, ZIP codes and notes are optional but help movers quote accurately.

  2. A person reads it

    We make sure it is a genuine move in the Denver area and not spam.

  3. Up to three movers receive it

    Each one passed our standard before getting a request: an active Colorado PUC household-goods permit (USDOT and MC numbers too, when a move leaves the state), insurance, and a Better Business Bureau A+. We do the lookups by hand.

  4. They contact you

    Expect calls or texts, since you gave consent. They should offer a written estimate. Compare, ask questions, hire one or none.

What it costs you

Nothing. You never owe anything for using the form, and you are under no obligation to hire any mover that contacts you.

What we will not do

  • Promise a price. Movers set those.
  • Rank companies or show star scores.
  • Send your details to more than three movers.
  • Move anything ourselves.

Stopping the calls

Tell any caller to stop, reply STOP to a text, or email zach@eyetoad.com to have your request deleted.
