Privacy Policy

Updated November 11, 2026. Local Mover Denver is run by Eye To Ad Media of Denver, Colorado (“we”).

What you give us

Whatever you type in the request form: name, phone, optional email, ZIP codes, home size, target date and notes. We also store the date, time and page, the terms version and the exact consent sentence you saw, as proof of what you agreed to.

What we do with it

  • Read it and forward it to up to three partner moving companies.
  • Contact you about the request ourselves.
  • Keep records that help us block spam and answer legal questions.

We do not sell your details to data brokers and this site runs no advertising trackers or analytics scripts of its own.

Partner movers

They are separate businesses with their own policies. We ask them to use your details only for your move and to stop when you say so.

Calls and texts

Only if you tick the box. Texts and calls may use automated technology. Reply STOP to texts, or tell callers to remove you.

Retention and deletion

Requests are kept up to 24 months and then deleted. Email zach@eyetoad.com at any time to see, fix or erase your information. We respond to such requests from anyone, in Colorado or elsewhere.

Security

The form travels over HTTPS to our lead server. Details never go in web addresses.

Kids

For adults only; we do not knowingly collect data from under-18s.

Updates

Changes will be reflected in the date at the top.

Local Mover Denver is a free referral service operated by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado; not a moving company. We send your request to movers we checked, and you decide whether to hire anyone.
